Taste of Muar

Flavours and iconic dishes of Muar

Muar, a historic town in Johor, is widely known as a food haven filled with unique and authentic traditional dishes. From the rich and tangy Muar Asam Pedas, the fragrant Otak-otak, to Javanese-inspired favourites like Nasi Ambeng and Mee Bandung Muar, each dish reflects the culture, heritage, and vibrant flavours of the local community.
