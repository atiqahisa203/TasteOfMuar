Mee Siput

Mee Siput Muar is a famous traditional snack from Muar, Johor, known for its unique spiral shape that resembles a snail shell, making it easily recognizable. It is made from a dough of wheat flour that is manually shaped before being deep fried until crispy and golden. Mee Siput is usually enjoyed with fragrant sweet–spicy sambal tumis that enhances its flavour. The combination of the crunchy noodles and the spicy sambal makes this snack popular, whether eaten on its own or served as a side dish. Mee Siput Muar is not just a light snack, but also a culinary heritage of Johor that remains beloved to this day.

Mee siput Recipe

Bahan-bahan:

1 mangkuk lada kering (kisar halus).

5 ulas bawang putih (kisar halus).

2 biji bawang besar (kisar halus).

2 biji bawang holand (hiris kasar).

Senggenggam ikan bilis.

Belacan (sebesar ibu jari).

Asam jawa.

Gula melaka (1 ketul).

Mee siput (1 bungkus.

Cara Penyediaan:

  1. Panaskan minyak dan tumis bawang holand sampai kekuningan.
  2. Masukkan belacan dan air asam jawa. Dan sambung tumis.
  3. Perasakan dengan garam dan gula secukupnya.
  4. Masak sambal sehingga garing.
  5. Panaskan api lagi untuk goreng mee siput.
