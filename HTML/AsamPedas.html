Asam Pedas

Asam Pedas Muar is an iconic dish from the district of Muar, Johor, well-known for its thick gravy with a balanced spicy and sour taste. Made using a blend of ground dried chilies, tamarind, and traditional ingredients such as torch ginger flower, Vietnamese coriander (daun kesum), and fresh turmeric, Asam Pedas Muar stands out with its fragrant aroma and bold flavour. Usually cooked with stingray, mackerel, or tenggiri, this dish is loved by many for its fresh and authentic taste. Asam Pedas Muar is not only satisfying but also reflects the uniqueness of Johor cuisine, which is rich in tradition and flavour.

Asam Pedas Recipe

Bahan-bahan:

Bahan kisar:

2 biji bawang merah besar

4 ulas bawang putih

11/2 inci halia

1 inci kunyit hidup

1 sudu kecil lada hitam

Sedikit belacan (ikut rasa masing-masing tak letak pun tak apa)

Bahan lain:

Ikan apa-apa jenis

1 senduk penuh cili kering

1/2 cawan air asam jawa pekat

Daun kesum

1-2 kuntum bunga kantan

1 cawan air biasa

Garam secukupnya

Cara Penyediaan:

  1. Panaskan minyak masukkan bahan kisar tadi, tumis sampai naik bau.
  2. Masukkan cili kering dan tumis sampai pecah minyak.
  3. Bila bahan tumis tadi pecah minyak masukkan air asam jawa, tunggu smpai mendidih.
  4. Masukkan bunga kantan atau daun kesum dan masak sampai naik bau.
  5. Akhir sekali baru masukkan 1 ekor ikan yang telah dikerat dan garam secukup rasa.
